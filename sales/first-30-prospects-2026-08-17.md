# Verdict — first 30 outbound prospects

Research date: 2026-08-17

Offer: fixed-scope, asynchronous Traffic Waste Audit. One source, one agreed schema, up to 10M rows, executive PDF, evidence CSV, ranked sources/placements and estimated at-risk spend. No production credentials and no call required to start.

## How to use this list

- Send only 5–8 carefully personalized messages per working day.
- Address the named person, but use only the company's public contact form, public business email, or a normal LinkedIn connection request.
- Verify the person's current role on the day of sending.
- Do not attach a large file in the first message. Offer the sample-report link after interest or include one plain link at most.
- Do not promise “fraud detection”. Position the work as explainable traffic-quality and spend-efficiency analysis.
- Never ask for production credentials. Start from a field list or five anonymized rows.

Priority meaning:

- **A** — send in the first wave; strong fit and approachable decision-maker.
- **B** — good fit, but larger company, broader service mix, or likely internal analytics capability.
- **C** — publisher/AdTech partnership target; use the partner version, not the agency sales message.

Thanks — the next step is deliberately small. Please send only:

- file format and approximate row count;
- available event types;
- date range;
- whether spend/win price and conversions are included;
- five anonymized example rows or just the field names.

Please do not send production credentials or personal user data. We will confirm mapping, retention/deletion, fixed scope, price and acceptance criteria in writing before any full transfer.

## Commercial guardrails

- Founding-client range: USD 500–750 for one clearly bounded audit.
- Do not go below USD 500 unless the dataset is materially smaller and the reduced scope is written down.
- 50% before processing, 50% on delivery, using a lawful payment method agreed before work begins.
- One correction round is included; new data or a changed schema is a new scope.
- The full audit is never a free test. A free feasibility check is limited to field names or five anonymized rows.
- Audit fee can be credited toward an approved 30-day Verdict pilot.
