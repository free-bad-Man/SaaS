## Reply after interest

Thanks - the next step is deliberately small.

Please send only:

- file format and approximate row count;
- available event types;
- date range;
- whether spend/win price and conversions are included;
- five anonymized example rows or a field list.

Do not send production credentials. We will confirm the mapping, fixed scope, transfer method, retention window, price, and acceptance criteria in writing before receiving the full file.
