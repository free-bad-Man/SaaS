# Verdict Traffic Waste Audit

## Commercial promise

Turn 7-30 days of advertising traffic into an evidence-backed list of sources to keep, review, or pause - without granting production write access.

## Ideal client

- Performance agency, affiliate/CPA network, ad network, mobile app, or advertiser.
- At least USD 50,000 in monthly media spend or a meaningful source-quality problem.
- Can export JSON, JSONL, CSV, or a documented API report.
- Wants source-level evidence rather than a black-box "fraud percentage."

## Client provides

- One traffic source or buying platform.
- 7-30 days of impression/click/event data.
- Spend or win-price fields when financial impact is required.
- Postbacks/conversions when attribution and ROAS are in scope.
- A short data dictionary if field names are non-standard.

Production credentials are not required for the audit. Preferred transfer is an expiring object-storage upload link or SFTP into an isolated project directory. Files are encrypted in transit, fingerprinted on receipt, access-limited to the engagement, and deleted after acceptance or the agreed retention window.

## Deliverables

1. Input validation and source-quality summary.
2. ALLOW / WATCH / BLOCK distribution with explicit reason codes.
3. Ranked source and placement findings.
4. Estimated at-risk spend when cost data is supplied.
5. Evidence CSV for the scored rows in scope.
6. Executive PDF with a prioritized action plan.
7. Written handoff and one clarification round.

The audit reports configured evidence and recommendations. It does not claim that every blocked request is a bot or that fraud is legally proven.

## Fixed scope and acceptance

- Up to one source, one currency, and one agreed schema.
- Initial target: up to 10 million rows; larger files are quoted separately.
- Typical delivery: 3-5 business days after valid data is received.
- Acceptance: supplied files are processed, agreed metrics reconcile, report and evidence export open correctly, and each recommendation contains a reason code.
- One correction round is included for calculation or mapping defects.
- New data sources, schema changes, custom connectors, or new detection rules are additional scope.

## Pricing

- First reference engagements: USD 500-750.
- Standard audit after reference case: USD 1,000-1,500.
- 50% to start, 50% on delivery.
- The audit fee may be credited toward an approved Verdict platform pilot.

## Upsell path

1. Traffic Waste Audit.
2. 30-day shadow-mode pilot from USD 5,000.
3. Recurring platform subscription and managed monitoring.
4. Optional connector delivery and approved buying actions.

## Sales qualification questions

1. Which platform or source produced the traffic?
2. Which event types are available: bid requests, impressions, clicks, conversions, postbacks?
3. What time range and approximate row count are available?
4. Are spend/win price and source/placement identifiers included?
5. What decision should the report enable: refund evidence, source exclusion, budget reallocation, or ongoing monitoring?
